# Translation fidelity

The original controls meaning, order and identifiers. Approved terminology refines wording without overriding meaning. Machine translation is a draft aid, not authority. Preserve conditions, exceptions, negation, causality, comparisons, numbers, units, symbols, revision labels, enumerations and normative force. Preserve a suspected source error with a disposition; only a scoped actual decision authorizes correction.

Align source and target units. Independently author source expectations with source evidence and hashes before checking the target. Do not infer expected values from the translation. Numeric tokens are ordered and case-sensitive units/identifiers are distinct. A legitimate translation reordering currently fails the conservative numeric comparison and needs explicit investigation, not disabled checks.

Mechanics cannot adjudicate meaning, normative strength or all terminology. Review those in context. A sample report states only the selected pages; it never establishes full-source coverage. Source extraction that omits images or unsupported layers blocks the corresponding result. Do not claim a formal publication or legal/regulatory approval.
